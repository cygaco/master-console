### Deep Read

- **What emerged:** every watcher in the harbour was *attached to a ship*. Not one was attached
  to the *harbour*. The instrumentation was complete and the coverage was zero, because coverage
  was defined per-object and the failure lived in the space between objects. The lamp is bright
  and it is aimed backwards.
- **Hidden tension:** the eyes close *on success*. That is the cruel part — the watcher's
  termination condition is the good outcome, so the system is blindest immediately after it has
  done something right. A 40-minute silence right after a clean merge is exactly the silence
  nothing is built to hear.
- **Subconscious learning:** liveness is a property of the **arc**, not of the **step**. Any
  supervision scheme whose units are steps will have the same hole, whatever the steps are. The
  detector has to be armed by the thing that *starts the sprint* and disarmed by the thing that
  *ends the sprint* — nothing in between may turn it off. (Filed tonight as ED-428.)
- **Carry-forward image:** *the eye that closes on success.* Look for it wherever a monitor's
  lifetime is scoped to the task it monitors.

- **What emerged:** the defect has no villain. Both doors answered *their own* question

  sprint, in four different components, with four different pairs of questions.
- **Hidden tension:** the permissive answer wins not by policy but by *shape*. Nobody decided

  rule (P-136) arriving from a new direction.
- **Subconscious learning:** whenever a list is reused, ask *what question was this list built to
  answer* — and if the new caller is asking a different one, the answer is a new field, never a

- **Cross-link to dream 1:** both dreams are the same animal. Dream 1 is coverage defined per

  the space between predicates. **The gap is always at the join, and the join is always the thing
  nobody owns.**

## Speculative problem-solving (3 approaches to the stall)

- **Inversion:** instead of detecting silence, make progress *emit*. If the conductor is required
  to write a heartbeat row it must justify, absence becomes a positive failure rather than a
  non-event. (This is the ED-069 started-row mechanism reaching for a second use.)
- **Analogy:** a dead-man's switch on a train. The driver must keep pressing; stopping pressing
  *is* the brake. The sprint has no pedal — every current signal is a thing the system says when
  it is fine, never a thing it must keep saying.
- **Elimination:** drop the hardest constraint — that the detector must know what "progress" means.
  It does not. Any of {new commit, new dispatch row, sprint-progress mtime} moving is enough; the
  detector never has to understand the work, only that the disk changed.

The elimination framing is the cheapest and is what tonight's ED-428 candidate enforcer names.
