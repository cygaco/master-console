## The example (verbatim substance)

**Visual hierarchy** = arrangement of elements by importance, guiding the eye logically through a
design; determines what the user notices first, second, last. Backbone of graphic/web/UI design.

### Core techniques (manipulated visual properties)
- **Size & Scale** — larger commands attention; a three-tier system (Hook / Secondary Detail / Finisher).
- **Color & Contrast** — bright/saturated/contrasting pops; muted/tonal recedes.
- **Typography** — font weight/size/style (H1 vs body) creates an organized reading path.
- **Spacing & Alignment** — white space isolates + prevents overload; alignment groups related info.

### The "Three Flow" rule (plan the eye's path)
1. **Entry Point (Hook)** — what draws you in first (main image, big headline, hero product photo).
2. **Secondary Details** — where the eye goes next (benefit, date, context that sustains engagement).
3. **Finisher (CTA)** — where the gaze settles (Buy Now button, contact, location).

### Why it matters
Without hierarchy → confusing maze; "if everything stands out, nothing stands out." Good hierarchy
→ readability (scan in seconds), drives action (directs to CTAs/links).
