## R3 — how the cascade result is written, and why the wording matters

ε claimed the record-trust failure was downstream of the capture defect. β required it be marked UNPROVEN.
Lane F measured it and the claim was true.

**It is recorded as "true-but-unmeasured → measured", never as "the caution was unnecessary."** β's reason,
adopted: being right without evidence and being right with it are different states, and only the second can be
built on. A cycle where doubt is retroactively judged unnecessary because the guess was lucky is a cycle that
stops doubting.

## R4 — a measurement's environment is part of the measurement
