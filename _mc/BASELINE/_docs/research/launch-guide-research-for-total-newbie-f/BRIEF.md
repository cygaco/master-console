## Research Question
Launch-guide research for total-newbie founders shipping their first mobile/web product (2026). Three workstreams, equal weight: (1) GROWTH LOOPS — in-app review prompting (post-reward "enjoying the app?" modal routes yes to the native store review prompt, no to a private feedback form; verify 2026 Apple App Store Review Guidelines + Google Play policy compliance of this gating pattern, SKStoreReviewController/Play In-App Review API quotas and mechanics, trigger/frequency-cap best practices) AND referral programs (share-link + deferred deep-link attribution, single vs double-sided rewards, reward type by product economics, minimum viable fraud prevention for a solo founder, realistic k-factor expectations, when referrals are premature vs worth building). (2) ONBOARDING — signup-wall placement and progressive profiling: optimize time-to-problem-solved, guest/anonymous mode + account linking (Firebase/Supabase/Clerk mechanics + footguns), decision framework for which info to collect at signup vs gate at point-of-need, special constraints for teen/minor audiences (COPPA/GDPR-K, age gates), activation metrics + target bands. (3) TESTING MOBILE APPS ON A WINDOWS PC — 2026 toolchain by stack (Expo/React Native, Flutter, PWA, native Android emulator acceleration on Windows; the no-iOS-simulator-on-Windows problem: physical iPhone + Expo Go, EAS cloud builds, BrowserStack/Firebase Test Lab/device farms, Mac-in-cloud), what cannot be validated on emulator and needs a real device before launch, minimum physical device kit for a solo founder, Windows-specific traps (Hyper-V/AVD acceleration, WSL2 ports, USB drivers, firewall vs Metro dev server).

## Research Phases
### Landscape
Survey the current state — what exists, who are the key players, what approaches are in use

**Stop when:** 3+ distinct approaches identified
### Mechanics
How does it actually work — implementation details, code patterns

**Stop when:** 2+ real implementations found
### Failure Modes
What breaks — post-mortems, edge cases, limitations

**Stop when:** 5+ failure modes documented
### Contrarian
Why the premise might be wrong — alternatives and criticisms

**Stop when:** 2+ counter-arguments found
